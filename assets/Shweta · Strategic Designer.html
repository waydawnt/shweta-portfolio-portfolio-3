Hi, I'm Shweta.

I'm a strategic designer from the National Institute of Design (Strategic Design Management), with a background in user-centred design.

I design the in-between: people & business

I keep people at the centre and the larger system in sight. I'm interested in how what people need and what a business wants pull on each other, and I work across research, strategy, and experience design to find where they can work together.

Currently interning on product strategy at a drone hardware company.
